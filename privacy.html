《图灵机谜题》（以下称"本游戏"）由 OceanSide Games 开发。我们不收集、不存储、也不分享你的任何个人信息。

我们不收集的数据

本游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

English

Turing Machine Puzzle · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Turing Machine Puzzle ("the game") is made by OceanSide Games. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What we don't collect

  • The game runs entirely on your device. It needs no internet connection, account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party services that collect data.
  • The game does not access your contacts, photos, location or any other information on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress, medals, drafts and settings (such as text size, volume and theme) are saved only on your own device and are never uploaded. Deleting the game deletes them too. You can also clear your progress in Settings → Reset progress.

Children's privacy

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
